Transcriber

Turns a recording into text on your own Mac. Nothing you record is uploaded anywhere.

Download for Mac

Version __LATEST_TAG__ · needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later

Setting it up

  1. Download it

    Click Download for Mac above. A file named like Transcriber-0.5.0-macos.dmg arrives in your Downloads folder.

  2. Put it in Applications

    Double-click the downloaded file. In the window that opens, drag the Transcriber icon onto the Applications folder beside it.

  3. Unblock it once, in Terminal

    Transcriber is not registered with Apple, so the first time macOS refuses to open it. One command, typed once, tells your Mac that you trust it.

    Open Terminal: press ⌘ and Space together, type Terminal, and press Return. Then copy this line, paste it into the Terminal window, and press Return:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Transcriber.app

    When it works, Terminal shows nothing at all; that is expected. You can close Terminal. Do this again only when you download a new version.

  4. Open Transcriber

    Open it from Applications like any other app. The first time, it downloads its speech model (465 MB) and shows the progress at the top of the window. After that it works without an internet connection.

Using it

  • Drop an audio or video file onto the window: mp3, m4a, wav, aiff, flac, mp4, mov and similar.
  • Play the recording in the app; the text follows along, and double-clicking a word jumps to that moment.
  • Search the text, save it as a .txt file, or save the whole session to reopen later.
  • If you run a local language model (LM Studio, Ollama or llama.cpp), Transcriber can also tidy the text into paragraphs and write a summary, still without anything leaving your Mac.
